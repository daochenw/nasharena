## Names are trademarks

A player's name always wears its owner's face, wherever it appears; the words
around it stay in the page's own hand (Newsreader, lowercase on plaques).
Never set a whole sentence in a name's face.

- Stockfish: 'Chakra Petch', 600 — machined.
- Leela, and the human seat on the play page: 'Great Vibes' — flowing.
- Everything else — verbs, outcomes, "a draw": the page serif.

So a verdict reads `<span class="who">Stockfish</span> wins`, not a plaque of
Chakra Petch. Same rule for the "play Stockfish" door and any new copy.
